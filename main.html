About

Where hardware and software have to meet in the middle

My work sits at the intersection of embedded systems, electronics, and the software that runs on top of them. I care about what happens at the boundary — where a sensor's analog signal becomes a firmware interrupt, and where that firmware becomes an interface someone actually uses.

I'd rather build a working prototype than a slide deck about one. Most of my projects start with a real, specific problem — a piano student who can't tell if their hand position is right, a phone that fits how someone actually wants to use one — and get solved by whatever combination of circuit design, firmware, and code the problem calls for.
